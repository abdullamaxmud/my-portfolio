4 SEASON: a point of sale built for a busy restaurant

A point-of-sale system for the 4 SEASON restaurant. Staff pick dishes from a visual menu, build the order and complete the sale in a few taps, on the restaurant’s own computers.

Role
Full-stack developer, UI design
Year

Status

The problemWhat I didResultWhat I'd do next

The problem

At busy times orders were written by hand and added up at the counter. Mistakes in totals and lost tickets cost money, and there was no record of what sold best.

What I did

  1. 01One menu, one source of truth. Dishes, categories and prices live in one database, so the cashier screen, the totals and the sales records always agree.
  2. 02Designed for speed at the counter. Large buttons, pictures for every dish and the running total always on screen, so a new cashier can take an order without training.
  3. 03Built the promotion. The “Order in Seconds” poster below tells customers about the faster service.

Result

Localruns on the restaurant’s own network
0:23demo video of a complete order

The restaurant takes orders on screen instead of on paper. Totals are calculated for the cashier, and every sale is saved, so the owner can see what sells.

What I'd do next

Next, I would add a kitchen display so orders reach the cooks instantly, and a simple daily report the owner can read on a phone.
